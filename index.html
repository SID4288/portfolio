        Applied AI, grounded in real data

        I’m a Computer Engineering student at Kathmandu University (CGPA 3.92/4.00) focused on machine learning, data engineering, and full-stack software. I co-authored a peer-reviewed paper in the Journal of Hydroinformatics on satellite-based flood mapping.

        Read the Paper
        Research

        Flood mapping from space

        Co-authored a paper on global flood extent mapping from Sentinel-1 SAR imagery with a UNet++ framework and permanent-water-augmented input, published in the Journal of Hydroinformatics.

        • Aquila: UNet++ reaching macro IoU 0.734 and macro F1 0.829 across 11 flood events (Sen1Floods11).
        • Benchmarked UNet, UNet++, ResNet34 and EfficientNet, with ESA WorldCover masks separating transient floodwater from permanent water.
        AI Projects

        Security and sound, built with models

        Zero Trust Agent Sandbox (Jun 2026) intercepts and approves every AI tool call before it runs, addressing OWASP LLM-07 and LLM-08, with Llama 3.2 via Ollama in a network-disabled Docker container.

        Cadence (Apr 2026) is a Nepali folk music genre classifier: a custom dataset and a hybrid EfficientNet-B0 + BiLSTM on mel-spectrograms, benchmarked against an SVM baseline.

        Data & Full-Stack

        Pipelines and platforms

        An F1 analytics ETL pipeline (in progress) pulled 188,000+ records from 2018–2025, validated at 96.5% lap-data and 92.6% pit-stop accuracy. CookeryKaa (2024) is a recipe platform with a normalized PostgreSQL schema, Django REST API, and React frontend.

        Also built: Aanubadh, MemoryPalace, Flow, SFaceX, StyleMe, and Pipeline-honesty.
